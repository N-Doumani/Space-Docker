Mind-Bending Facts About Reality

1

One Day on Venus is Longer Than Its Year

Venus rotates so sluggishly that a single rotation on its axis takes 243 Earth days, while orbiting the Sun only takes 225 Earth days!

2

A Teaspoon of a Neutron Star Weighs Billions of Tons

Neutron stars are so compressed that just one sugar-cube-sized portion of their matter would weigh approximately 1 billion metric tons on Earth.

3

Space is Entirely Silent

Because sound waves require a medium (like air or water) to travel through, the vacuum of space has no atmosphere to carry screams, explosions, or whispers.

The Moon's Distance
384,400 km

All 7 other major planets in our solar system could fit side-by-side inside the empty space between Earth and our Moon with room to spare!

The Sun's Dominance
99.86% Total Mass

The Sun contains 99.86% of all the mass in the entire solar system. Approximately 1.3 million Earths could fit inside its spherical volume.

Voyager 1's Journey
24+ Billion km

Launched in 1977, Voyager 1 is the farthest human-made object. A radio signal traveling at the speed of light still takes over 22 hours to reach it.
